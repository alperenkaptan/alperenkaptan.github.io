Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your Scrapyard Squad game account and associated personal data by email, without installing or opening the game. Android package: co.sproutquest.scrapyardsquad.

This is a manual request process. Opening this page or an email draft does not submit a request or delete anything. Send the email to submit your request. We will complete deletion within 30 days of receiving your request and notify you by email. If ownership verification or a legally permitted extension is needed, we will explain the reason and expected completion date by email.

How to submit your request

  1. Email info@sproutquest.co with the subject Scrapyard Squad account deletion.
  2. Tell us whether you played with Google or as a guest. If available, include your support ID from the game's Account → Copy support ID button. For a linked account, contacting us from its Google email address helps us locate it. You can still contact us if you no longer have access to the game or that email address.
  3. Send the message. We may ask for limited information to verify ownership before deleting data. A support ID alone is not proof of ownership. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, access tokens, or payment details.
Open deletion request email

If the button does not open your email app, copy the address and subject above into your preferred email service. Requests are tracked and handled through our support inbox.

What will be deleted?

After verification, we delete your Firebase game account and the associated data we can identify: cloud career statistics, robot and equipment purchase counts, saved winning teams, and linked replay files and metadata. You can also request deletion of identifiable analytics, diagnostic, or support data; we will explain which records can be linked to your account and the relevant provider deletion process. Aggregated or genuinely anonymous statistics cannot be attributed to an individual account.

Deletion of your game account does not delete your Google account. Uninstalling the game or signing out alone does not delete cloud records.

Local saves and retained records

We cannot remotely erase files on your device. After we confirm deletion, clear the game's app storage on each device and remove any game backups you control. Restoring an old backup or continuing to use the game can create a new account or upload locally retained progress again.

Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations, fraud prevention, or unresolved disputes. If an exception applies, we will tell you which records are retained, why, and for how long. Backup and service-provider deletion can follow their own cleanup schedules; see our privacy policy for existing retention periods. Providers acting independently handle their records under their own policies.
