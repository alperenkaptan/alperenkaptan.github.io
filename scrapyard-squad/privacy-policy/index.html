Privacy policy

Scrapyard Squad is a game published under the SproutQuest brand by an independent developer. This policy describes data used by the Android game co.sproutquest.scrapyardsquad and the services supporting it. For privacy questions or requests, email info@sproutquest.co.

Game progress and identifiers

Game progress, settings, and the expiry of rewarded speed access are stored on your device. The current game does not require you to supply your name, email address, or a social sign-in to play. It creates game/run identifiers and uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication for online replay services. These identifiers can link activity from an installation or run; they are pseudonymous, not a guarantee that the data is anonymous.

Gameplay analytics and diagnostics

When enabled in the game's privacy settings, Google Analytics for Firebase records gameplay and app events, such as run starts and ends, wins and losses, difficulty, shop actions, battle controls, app backgrounding, and rewarded-ad interactions. Events can include run identifiers, timestamps, app/build version, and game rules version. Google services also process installation/session identifiers, device and operating-system information, language, and approximate location derived from network information. We use these records to understand game balance, continued play, technical performance, and use of rewards.

Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports and diagnostic information when diagnostics are enabled, including stack traces, app version, device/OS information, installation identifiers, and relevant diagnostic logs. We use this information to investigate failures and improve stability.

Detailed gameplay replays

When replay sharing is enabled, the game uploads compressed gameplay snapshots through Firebase Cloud Functions to Cloud Storage, with ownership and upload records in Cloud Firestore. These snapshots contain game state, teams and items, shop decisions, opponents, battle randomness/results, run identifiers, action labels, and timestamps. They allow the developer to reconstruct a player's run for debugging and gameplay analysis and can be linked to analytics using run identifiers.

Replays are not screen recordings: they do not record video, microphone audio, camera images, or free-form chat. Uploaded replays are for developer analysis, not a public player gallery. Authentication, Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity, and upload limits help protect the service from abuse.

Rewarded advertisements

The game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads, including temporary access to 2× battle speed and an eligible once-per-run revival. Ad availability and the reward depend on the ad service and successful reward completion. We record ad availability, interaction, reward outcome, and revenue information for these placements.

Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses, device/app information, identifiers available under the app and platform settings, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. The current Android build disables advertising-ID collection through its manifest; other identifiers and network information may still be processed. Choosing not to watch an ad does not by itself stop all advertising SDK activity: eligible ads can be loaded in advance.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages applicable advertising privacy messages and choices. Ad requests are gated by the SDK's consent status. Declining consent may change the types or availability of ads; it does not necessarily mean no ads can be served. See Google's advertising information and Google's privacy policy.

Your choices

Current beta behavior: gameplay analytics, diagnostics, and detailed replay sharing are enabled by default when no saved choice exists. The beta does not automatically show a separate prompt for these three settings. You can change them in the game's Settings → Privacy. Existing opt-outs are preserved.

Turning replay sharing off removes pending local replay uploads; an upload already in progress may finish. Turning collection off does not automatically erase records already sent to service providers. Advertising consent choices are separate from analytics, diagnostics, and replay settings. Where required by UMP, an Ad Privacy option is available in the game's privacy settings to review advertising choices.

The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, phone-call, or precise GPS permissions. Platform privacy settings may provide additional advertising controls. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local data subject to Android's storage and backup behavior, but does not by itself delete previously uploaded records.

Service providers and processing locations

We use Google/Firebase services for analytics, crash reporting, anonymous authentication, backend processing, storage, integrity checks, and advertising. Network services receive technical request information such as IP addresses. Replay backend resources are configured in European regions; this does not mean every Google advertising, analytics, diagnostic, or support record is processed only in Europe. Providers may process data in other countries under their applicable terms and safeguards.

Read Firebase's privacy and security information and Google's privacy policy. This policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages with domain services provided through Cloudflare; those providers may process technical access logs. This page contains no advertising or analytics scripts.

Retention and deletion requests

Local progress remains on your device until overwritten, reset, or removed. Replay storage is scheduled for deletion after 30 days, and backend replay metadata uses expiry-based cleanup. Cleanup is asynchronous, and recoverable storage copies can remain for an additional recovery period, currently seven days. Analytics data follows the configured Google Analytics retention controls; aggregate reports may be retained separately. Google describes Crashlytics diagnostic retention as 90 days before removal from live and backup systems begins. Provider security, authentication, and operational records follow the relevant service retention rules.

To request access, correction, or deletion of identifiable game data, email info@sproutquest.co with the subject Scrapyard Squad data request. State what you want deleted or reviewed and include any game/run reference already available to you. You can contact us without access to the app; do not send passwords or payment details.

Requests are handled manually. We may ask for limited information needed to locate the relevant pseudonymous records and verify your request. We cannot reliably identify an installation from an email address alone because the game does not require an email login. We will explain what we can locate, the deletion process, and any records that must be retained for security, legal obligations, or resolving disputes. Provider records held independently have their own request mechanisms.

Support, children, and changes

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We retain correspondence as needed to address the request and maintain relevant support records. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

We do not ask players to provide a date of birth or knowingly request children's personal information. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and address it. Rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, consent withdrawal, or a complaint to a privacy authority depend on the law applicable to you.

We may update this policy as the game and its services change. The date above identifies the latest version; material changes will be communicated where required.
